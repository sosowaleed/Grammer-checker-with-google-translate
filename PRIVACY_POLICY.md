# Privacy Policy for PolyglotGrammar

**Effective Date:** September 8, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 8, 2026  

PolyglotGrammar ("the Extension", "we", "us", or "our") is a browser extension developed by sosowaleed to provide real-time multilingual grammar proofreading, contextual synonyms discovery, and translation across 130+ languages.

We are committed to respecting and protecting user privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines what information the Extension processes, how it is handled, and your rights.

---

## 1. Information We Process

### A. Website Content (Text Input & Selection)
- **What is accessed:** The Extension accesses editable text fields (such as `<input>`, `<textarea>`, and `contenteditable` elements) and user-selected text on web pages solely when the user interacts with the page or activates the Extension.
- **Purpose:** To analyze text for grammatical and spelling errors, provide contextual synonym suggestions, and perform on-demand translations.
- **Transient Processing:** Text is processed in real time. We do **not** store, record, or log any text you type on any private or proprietary developer servers.

### B. Third-Party Services (Google Translate)
- When a spellcheck or translation lookup is performed, text segments are transmitted via encrypted HTTPS connections directly to Google Translate endpoints (`https://*.google.com/*` and `https://*.googleapis.com/*`) to fetch linguistic corrections, definitions, and translations.
- Such processing is subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### C. Local Preferences & Settings (chrome.storage)
- The Extension uses browser local storage (`chrome.storage.local`) strictly on your local device to save:
  - User settings (e.g., auto-check toggle, interface language preference, preferred target language).
  - Custom ignored words and ignored website domains.
  - Short-term in-memory cache of recent translation queries to minimize network requests.
- This data never leaves your device and is never synchronized or uploaded to any external server by us.

---

## 2. Information We DO NOT Collect

- We do **NOT** collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as names, email addresses, physical addresses, phone numbers, or government IDs.
- We do **NOT** monitor, record, or track your browsing history or keystrokes outside the scope of real-time proofreading.
- We do **NOT** access password fields (`type="password"`), sensitive payment fields, or elements marked with `autocomplete="off"` or `data-gramm="false"`.
- We do **NOT** use tracking cookies, advertising trackers, or fingerprinting technologies.
- We do **NOT** sell, rent, or monetize any user data to third parties or data brokers.
- We do **NOT** use or transfer user data for creditworthiness or lending decisions.

---

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion

- All user settings and ignored lists are stored locally within your browser.
- You can clear all stored preferences at any time by uninstalling the Extension or clearing the browser extension storage.

---

## 4. Compliance with Chrome Web Store Policies

PolyglotGrammar adheres strictly to the **Google Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies**, including the **Limited Use requirements**:
- The Extension accesses user data solely to provide and improve user-facing features described in our single purpose description.
- We do not allow third parties to serve targeted advertisements based on user data.
- We do not use remote code; all scripts are bundled within the package.
